# Data dictionary — entirely fictional data

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| month | Month in YYYY-MM format |
| department | Fictional business department |
| category | Revenue or expense category |
| budget_eur | Simulated planned monthly amount in EUR |
| actual_eur | Simulated realized monthly amount in EUR |
| variance_eur | Actual minus budget |
| variance_pct | Variance as percentage of budget |

Positive variance is not always favorable: it depends on whether the category represents revenue or expense. This demo intentionally reports mathematical variances without applying business-specific favorability labels.
